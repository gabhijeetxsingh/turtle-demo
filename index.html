What is the Carbon Market?

\n

The carbon market is a system that allows countries and companies to trade carbon credits to reduce greenhouse gas emissions. It aims to provide economic incentives for reducing emissions and promoting sustainable practices.

\n
\n
\n

How Does It Work?

\n

Entities that reduce their emissions below a certain cap can sell their excess allowances to those that exceed their limits. This creates a financial incentive for companies to invest in cleaner technologies.

\n
\n
